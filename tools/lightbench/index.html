Lightbench

Project a sewing pattern at true size onto your cutting mat, one piece at a time, and keep track of what you've cut.

Launch Lightbench
You needA tablet or laptopiPad works best. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, for full screen.
You needA projector above the matSend the picture by screen mirroring or USB-C to HDMI. Bluetooth carries sound only.
You needA gridded cutting matIts printed grid is what Lightbench calibrates against.
You needA projector PDFA0 or projector files work best. Print-at-home pages can be put together.

How to use it

1

Calibrate to your mat

On the Mat tab, type the size of your mat or of a rectangle printed on it. Tap Match the corners and drag A, B, C and D onto its corners. Use the arrows for the last few pixels, then Save the setup so you only do this once.

[PHOTO · CORNERS ON MAT]
2

Open your pattern

On the Pattern tab, tap Open PDF. Untick sizes you aren't using. For print-at-home pages, use Put pages together and set the trim until the lines meet.

[SCREENSHOT · SIZES LIST]
3

Mark the pieces

Tap Mark pieces, then Find for me, or tap inside each piece. Shape takes the whole outline; Fill stops at the nearest line. Unmark anything that isn't a piece, then tap Done.

[SCREENSHOT · MARKED PIECES]
4

Open folds and line up the grain

Choose Line and draw along a place-on-fold line; it snaps onto the pattern. Tap Mirror on fold to see the whole piece. Draw along the grainline and tap Make this the grain to turn the piece square to the mat.

[SCREENSHOT · MIRRORED PIECE]
5

Cut and tick off

Untick Show the PDF sheet, then turn on Show one piece at a time. Drag the piece onto your fabric, turn it with two fingers or the 1° buttons, and tick it once it's cut. Press Q to black out the projector while you check your fabric.

[PHOTO · CUTTING ON THE MAT]

Good to know

Your patterns stay on your device. Projects and calibration are saved in this browser only. Clearing website data clears them.

Screen changed size? Lightbench asks you to recheck the corners, since the grid may no longer match the mat.

Zoomed out? A red note at the bottom means you're not at true size. Tap it to go back.
